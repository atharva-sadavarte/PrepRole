LEGAL COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy for PrepRole

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 • Application: PrepRole (com.preprole)

1. Introduction

Welcome to PrepRole ("the App"). PrepRole is an AI-powered career coaching, ATS resume evaluation, and interview preparation mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring full transparency regarding how your data is collected, processed, and safeguarded.

By using PrepRole, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not access or use the App.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Details: When signing in with Google OAuth, we receive your full name, email address, profile picture URL, and Google user identifier. We never receive or store your Google password.
  • Resume & Career Files: Resumes, CVs, job descriptions, target roles, and career goals uploaded to generate ATS scoring, skill gap analysis, and bullet-point rewrites.
  • Audio & Voice Data: When using the AI Mock Interviewer or Pitch Coach, the App requests microphone access to record your spoken answers to provide speech pacing, tone, and conversational feedback. Audio is not used for biometric identification.
  • Camera Access (Optional): During video-assisted mock interview practice, the camera is used strictly for an in-app local preview to simulate a realistic video interview. Video feeds are processed entirely on-device and are never recorded, streamed, or stored on our servers.

B. Purchase & Financial Information

Zero Financial Data Retention: PrepRole processes in-app purchases and subscriptions through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We never collect, see, or store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking credentials.

We only receive non-sensitive purchase tokens, product IDs (e.g., preprole_pro_monthly), and expiration timestamps to activate and manage your Pro access.

C. Automatically Collected Device & Diagnostic Data

  • Device model, OS version, app version, network connectivity status, and crash logs to troubleshoot issues and maintain app stability.
  • Your unique Supabase user ID and scan quota counters stored securely in our database.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Analyze resume text and benchmark against ATS standards and target job descriptions.
  • Generate personalized AI coaching, tailored interview questions, and real-time response evaluations via Google Gemini AI.
  • Manage scan credits and maintain your Pro subscription across devices.
  • Authenticate user accounts securely via Google OAuth.
  • Diagnose bugs, optimize application speed, and improve user experience.

AI Ethics & Training Protection: Your uploaded resume data and interview responses are processed transiently via secure, encrypted serverless functions to return your score and recommendations. Your data is never sold and is NOT used to train public machine learning or foundation AI models.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement stringent security standards to safeguard your information:

  • Encryption: All data in transit is protected using TLS/SSL (HTTPS). Stored documents and database records are encrypted at rest.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Our database strictly restricts read/write permissions so that each user can only ever access their own resumes and analysis results.
  • User-Controlled Deletion: You can delete any saved resume or score report from your History screen, which permanently removes the document from our storage buckets.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full sovereignty over your data:

  • Access & Portability: View your analyses and score history anytime within the App.
  • Deletion ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Delete individual analyses anytime from the History tab.
  • Complete Account Deletion: To permanently delete your entire account, profile, and all associated documents, send an email to support@preprole.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We process complete data expungement within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

PrepRole is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided us with their details, contact us immediately at support@preprole.com for immediate deletion.
